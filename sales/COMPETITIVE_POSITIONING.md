## vs. WhatsApp groups + Google Form

Some city operators ask why they need a platform at all when they could run a
group with referrals. Honest answers:

- WhatsApp groups don't scale past ~150 craftsmen and ~500 customers. You become
  the operations bottleneck.
- There's no automated commission collection — you chase craftsmen for cash.
- There's no rating system, so a bad craftsman keeps getting work.
- You can't show investors anything when you decide to raise.

If your city is genuinely tiny (under 50,000 population) and the goal is a
side-hustle, a WhatsApp group + Google Form may be the right MVP. Come back to
us when you cross 200 monthly bookings.

## vs. Hiring an agency to build a custom app

Agencies in Egypt typically charge 500,000 - 2,000,000 EGP for a
"customer + provider marketplace app" and ship in 9-15 months. The result is:

- A version 1 product with no marketplace-specific learning baked in
- No mobile app maintenance plan
- No Arabic/Egyptian UX tuning beyond what you specified up-front
- Code quality varying wildly by which contractor they assigned

Sana3y is a product, not a project. The product has been used, debugged, and
hardened. The license is cheaper than the agency and ships in days.

## When *not* to buy a Sana3y license

We sell to people we think will succeed. If any of the following are true,
**don't buy from us yet**:

- You don't live in (or have deep local relationships in) the target city
- You don't have 50,000 - 150,000 EGP of marketing runway after the license
- You can't commit at least 20 hours/week to running the operation for 6 months
- You're not willing to do KYC reviews and dispute resolution yourself
- You see this as a passive investment

A reseller who buys 5 city licenses and tries to run all 5 with no local
ground game will fail. We have seen it. We will not sell to operators who
can't make the city work.
