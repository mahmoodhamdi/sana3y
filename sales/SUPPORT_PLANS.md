# Support Plans

Every license tier includes a baseline support period. After that period you
can renew annually, downgrade, or self-maintain.

## What's included in each plan

| Plan | Bundled with | Duration | Channels | Response SLA | Includes |
|------|--------------|---------:|----------|--------------|----------|
| **Bronze** | Starter | 3 months | Email | 48 hours | Bug fixes for product defects, security patches, dependency updates that we ship to the main product |
| **Silver** | Pro | 6 months | Email + WhatsApp | 24 hours | Bronze + minor enhancements (up to 4 hours of dev work per month) |
| **Gold** | Enterprise | 12 months | Dedicated channel + monthly review call | 4 hours (business hours), 24 hours (outside) | Silver + 1 major feature per quarter (up to 24 hours dev) + 99.5% uptime SLA on managed hosting |
| **Platinum** | Regional Master | 24 months | Dedicated engineer (25% allocation), Slack Connect | 2 hours (business hours), 8 hours (outside) | Gold + custom roadmap input + 99.9% uptime SLA |

## What "bug fix" means

A bug is behaviour that materially differs from the documented or expected
behaviour. Examples:

- ✅ The OTP screen doesn't dismiss after a successful verification.
- ✅ Craftsman wallet shows a balance higher than the sum of completed jobs.
- ✅ Geo-search returns no results within 5 km despite seeded craftsmen.
- ✅ The admin dashboard crashes when filtering by date.

The following are **not** bugs and are billed as enhancement requests:

- 💰 "I want a different color on the home screen."
- 💰 "Add a new payment provider not on our roadmap."
- 💰 "Translate the app into French."
- 💰 "Integrate with my accounting software."

## What we ship to support customers automatically

While your support is active, you receive:

- Patch releases (security fixes, dependency CVE mitigations) within 7 days of
  upstream release
- Minor releases (small improvements, performance work) ~monthly
- A monthly newsletter listing what changed and any breaking notices

Major releases (Sana3y v2.x) are **opt-in** — we do not auto-migrate you. We
publish a migration guide and you choose when to upgrade. Migration work is
billed at preferential rates for support-active customers.

## Response time clarification

"Response SLA" means the time from you opening a ticket to a human engineer
acknowledging it and starting work. It does not guarantee a fix in that time —
complex bugs can take longer. We commit to a clear estimate in the first response.

Business hours: Sunday–Thursday, 09:00–17:00 Cairo time. Saturday afternoon and
Friday are non-working unless your plan includes 24/7 (Platinum only).

## What if support lapses

If you let support lapse and later need help:

- You can still call us, but the engagement is billed as a one-off project.
- Bug-fix-only re-engagement: 15,000 EGP, gets you a 30-day window.
- Full reactivation: 50% of the equivalent renewal price + a 50% surcharge,
  brings you back to the original plan for 12 months.

## What we don't do

- We do not run your customer support. End-customer tickets ("my plumber didn't
  show up") are yours to handle.
- We do not market or recruit craftsmen for you.
- We do not provide call centers, KYC operations staff, or dispute mediators.
- We do not manage your social media or app store presence.
