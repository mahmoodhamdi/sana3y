## How payment works

- 50% on signing the license agreement
- 50% on production deployment + acceptance test sign-off
- Acceptance criteria are defined in [`HANDOVER_CHECKLIST.md`](./HANDOVER_CHECKLIST.md)
- Currencies accepted: EGP (bank transfer / InstaPay), SAR (bank transfer), USD/EUR
  (bank transfer / Wise)
- Invoice issued upon each payment; Egyptian VAT applied if you are inside Egypt
  and registered

## What happens if you don't launch

License fees are non-refundable once we ship the source code. If launch is
delayed for any reason, your support period starts when you take production
delivery, not when you sign — you don't lose months of support to a
deployment delay.
